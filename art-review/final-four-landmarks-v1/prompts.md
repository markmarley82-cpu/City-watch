Shared request: repaint the blockout as a detailed, weathered, hand-painted City Watch sprite, using its geometry and the approved references' palette, materials and dark outlines. Projection edits preserved the designs and requested only geometric correction: side foundation endpoints level, central front/bottom corner lower by one quarter the total foundation width, verticals unchanged and corresponding roof/courses/sills aligned to the same axes. Bakery took two projection edits, shrine and alley one each; the original outpost fell within tolerance. Final measured results are in registration.json; prompts alone are not evidence of exact geometry.

Night edits, independently for each selected day source: change only illumination to cool blue moonlight on upper edges/roofs with warm amber light confined to existing windows/lanterns/candles; retain architecture, silhouette, framing and transparent background; no new objects, ground, sky or external bloom.
