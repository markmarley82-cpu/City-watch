## Defects found

### P2 — Duty points to Colon when Carrot is the active objective

1. Complete the paid Guild licence patrol and file it with Colon. The test save reached Constable, 19 merit and 9 coins, at Watch Square [121,134].
2. With the objective saying Captain Carrot has an assignment outside the Watch House, open **Duty → Walk to current assignment**.
3. The game says “You are at the approach,” stays at [121,134], and offers **Talk to Sergeant Colon**. Opening **Carrot’s assignment** directly from that location describes going east but only offers **Return to street**.

**Expected:** The walk action takes the player to Carrot’s approach and offers the correct interaction. **Workaround verified:** Tap the Dis-organiser twice, choose **Walk to Captain Carrot**, and speak to him at [134,135].

### P2 — Reload restores case progress but shows the wrong objective

1. Accept **The Missing Night Delivery** from Carrot. Use **Map → Mended Drum → Walk here**, then question the carter at [107,243]. The objective advances to “Follow another lead, check the receiving yard, or prepare a report.”
2. Reload the live page. This test also had an open Mended Drum encounter.
3. Mended Drum position, 19 merit, 9 coins, the **Resume encounter** button and the Notebook’s **Investigating · 1/3 leads** all returned. The street objective instead said “Report to Sergeant Colon.”

**Expected:** The street objective should reflect the saved case and lead count. **Impact:** Progress did not disappear, but the street guidance makes the saved investigation look inactive. The Notebook still shows the correct case state. Reproduction without an open encounter remains to be checked.

### Usability — active leads take extra navigation

Carrot’s Duty panel lists the Mended Drum carter, Dock Warehouse ledger and Crust Hall baker as plain text. The atlas provides a working route. Consider adding a walk action for each lead while preserving the choice of which to pursue first.
