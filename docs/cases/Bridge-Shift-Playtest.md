# Playtest: The Bridge That Was Closed (CW-NC-01 pilot)

**Goal:** find out whether two players make different reasonable choices, see
different consequences, and both feel they did good police work. If everyone
follows the same marker and picks the obvious answer, revise the case before
building the next one.

This is the team's pilot (`dist/cases/bridge-closure.mjs`), set at Brass
Bridge, with Times and Inquirer follow-ups.

## Setup (for the person running the test)

- Play at https://markmarley82-cpu.github.io/City-watch/ on **desktop** and on
  an **iPhone** (Safari). Use a player who has finished the opening purse case.
- The case appears at the top of **Duty** as **The Bridge That Was Closed**.
  It needs Nobby Nobbs or Sergeant Colon as patrol partner (Colon joins at
  6 merit; change partner in Duty → Patrol partner before taking the case).
- Walk to a stop with the button in the case screen. On arrival a **✦ button**
  appears on the street; tapping it opens the case at that step. (Opening
  **Duty → The Bridge That Was Closed** there works too.)
- The case is played once per save. To test both partners, use two fresh
  saves (or two testers).
- After filing, play on to the next night and buy the Times or the Inquirer:
  the case should be reported, and a second notice at Contract Bridge appears
  as a follow-up lead.

## Rules for the tester

- **Don't coach.** Don't say where to go first, which partner to take, or what
  to file. If they ask, say "do what you think a Watch officer would do".
- Let them read at their own pace. Note where they hesitate.
- Ask the questions below **after** they file the report, not during.

## Questions to ask afterwards

1. Which partner did you take, and why?
2. Where did you go first: the bridge notice or the dispatch clerk? Why?
3. At the bridge, did you reopen the passage straight away or watch first?
   What did you expect to happen? Did it?
4. What did you think your evidence actually proved?
5. Which report did you file, and why that one?
6. Did the debrief feel fair? Did you feel you did good police work?
7. Did you notice the newspaper follow-up? Did it make the case feel bigger?
8. If you played again, would you choose a different partner or a different
   call at the bridge? What would you hope to find?
9. Was anything confusing, too slow, or hard to tap on the phone?

## What each path can prove (for the reviewer, not the tester)

| Partner | Bridge call | Evidence you can get | Strongest report |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nobby | Remove the notice now | notice, tally | Passage reopened, organiser unproved (+2 merit) |
| Nobby | Watch first (Nobby blends into the queue) | collector, then the signed route order | Organiser proved, with the tally (+4 merit) |
| Colon | Colon keeps the lane clear while you remove it | public confirmation, then the driver's declaration with Colon at the dispatch clerk | Organiser proved, with the tally (+4 merit) |
| Colon | Watch first (Colon stays beside you) | collector | Passage reopened, collection witnessed (+2 merit) |

## Milestone

Met when two players make different reasonable choices, see different
consequences, and both say they did good police work. Revise first if:
everyone picks the same partner and call; players file a report they cannot
explain; or the "organiser unproved" report feels like failing.
