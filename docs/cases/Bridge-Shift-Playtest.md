# Playtest: The Bridge That Was Closed (partner shift)

**Goal:** find out whether two players make different reasonable choices, see
different consequences, and both feel they did good police work. If everyone
follows the same marker and picks the obvious answer, revise the shift before
building the next case.

## Setup (for the person running the test)

- Play at https://markmarley82-cpu.github.io/City-watch/ on **desktop** and on
  an **iPhone** (Safari). Use a fresh save or a player who has finished the
  opening purse case.
- The shift is in **Duty → City cases → Shift: The Bridge That Was Closed**.
  It needs Nobby Nobbs or Sergeant Colon as patrol partner (Colon joins at
  6 merit; change partner in Duty → Patrol partner).
- To test again, work the shift again from City cases (merit is only paid the
  first time for each finding; a small wage is paid every run).

## Rules for the tester

- **Don't coach.** Don't say where to go first, which partner to take, or what
  to file. If they ask, say "do what you think a Watch officer would do".
- Let them read at their own pace. Note where they hesitate.
- Ask the questions below **after** they file the report, not during.

## Questions to ask afterwards

1. Which partner did you take, and why?
2. Where did you go first: the bridge notice or the quay clerk? Why?
3. At the bridge, did you take the notice down or leave it up? What did you
   expect to happen? Did it?
4. What did you think your evidence actually proved?
5. Which finding did you file, and why that one?
6. Did the debrief feel fair? Did you feel you did good police work?
7. If you played again, would you choose a different partner or a different
   call at the bridge? What would you hope to find?
8. Was anything confusing, too slow, or hard to tap on the phone?

## What each path can prove (for the reviewer, not the tester)

| Partner | Bridge call | Best possible finding | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nobby | Take it down now | Route reopened | Whoever was behind it stays away |
| Nobby | Leave it up and watch | Proved who paid (and who posted it) | Drivers kept waiting |
| Colon | Take it down now | Identified who posted it (with the quay clerk's sighting) | Whoever paid stays unknown |
| Colon | Leave it up and watch | Identified who posted it | Drivers waited, and the man was scared off by the uniform |

"Proved who paid" also needs the quay clerk's tally. "Identified who posted"
needs the clerk's dawn sighting plus either the sign-writer's mark (Colon) or
seeing the man in the apron (Nobby).

## Milestone

Met when two players make different reasonable choices, see different
consequences, and both say they did good police work. Revise first if:
everyone picks the same partner and call; players file a finding they cannot
explain; or the "honest partial" report feels like failing.
